## 6. Research findings

### There is no Thanksgiving meal to be had

Verified across official sources, food media, and community forums. **No Montreal restaurant, hotel, or venue offers a traditional Canadian Thanksgiving dinner on Oct 10–12, 2026.** Tourisme Montréal's own festive-dining roundup contains zero Action de grâce entries. Quebec treats l'Action de grâce as an autumn long weekend, not a turkey holiday.

- **Trap:** Alexandre et fils and Sir Winston Churchill Pub both serve full turkey dinners, but for **American** Thanksgiving in late November. They dominate search results.
- **Closed loop:** for a decade, forums pointed to **Le Montréalais** at the Queen Elizabeth as the one place doing it. That outlet no longer exists; Rosélys replaced it and has confirmed directly that it does not.
- **Booking risk, most important finding:** in Quebec the family celebration is **Sunday**, lunch or dinner. Independent restaurants have a documented pattern of closing Sunday and Monday of this weekend so staff can be with family, and those that open see heavier demand. **Do not assume normal Sunday service. Confirm open AND able to seat 11.**
- Weak leads only: **Marcus / Four Seasons** has stated intent for a Thanksgiving brunch edition, unpublished, and every precedent runs 9:00am–2:30pm, which collides with DodgeBow at 2:30. **Bar George / Le Mount Stephen** runs a standing All Day Sunday Roast, not a holiday menu; capacity and price unverified.
- Not checked: Reddit was blocked at the tool level in both passes. TripAdvisor's Montreal forum was the substitute.
