The Weekend

Saturday Evening “The Upgrade” +

Family heads out. Check into The Spectator. Let the butler take care of you. Dinner at Vern’s — Michelin 1-star, intimate candlelit, the date-night restaurant. Book on Resy — opens May 14 (30 days out). This is the night.

Sunday June 14 “Slow Day” +

Sleep in. Morning coffee at The Rise Coffee Bar (5 min walk, garden patio, most Instagrammable in Charleston) or Kudu (10 min, secret garden courtyard with fountain). Late morning: couples spa at The Dewberry or Woodhouse (see below). Brunch. Explore at your own pace. No agenda, no kids, no schedule. Dinner wherever sounds right — maybe Merci (26 seats, James Beard semifinalist) or FIG.

Monday June 15 “Depart” +

One last coffee — Bad Bunnies (8 min walk, perfect 5.0 Yelp, house-made everything, order the brown sugar iced coffee) or Harken Cafe (1 min from Mill House, biscuit sandwich + cortado). One last walk. Check out. Head to the airport.

The Spa

The Spectator doesn't have an on-site spa, but the butler will book everything for you

Top Pick

The Dewberry Spa

8-minute walk. Dedicated couples’ suite — you control music, lighting, climate. Cypress-covered walls, Natura Bissé products. Signature massage ~$215 for 90 min. Quiet luxury that matches The Spectator’s vibe.

843-872-9045 • Daily 9am–7pm

Full Experience

Woodhouse Spa at Wentworth Mansion

12-minute walk. Couples’ suite with aromatherapy steam shower. Set inside a Gilded Age mansion. Couples Retreat: $350–$700/person (50/80/110 min). The more indulgent option.

843-203-1772 • Daily 10:30am–5pm

Easy Option

In-Room Massage at The Spectator

No travel required. Ask the butler to arrange an in-room massage. Private, convenient, and you’re already in a robe. Contact them in advance for availability and pricing.

Ask your Spectator butler to pre-book — they recommend reaching out “as far in advance as possible.”
