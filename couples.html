The Weekend

Saturday Evening “The Upgrade” add

Family heads out. Check into The Spectator. Let the butler take care of you. Dinner at Vern’s — Michelin 1-star, intimate candlelit, the date-night restaurant. Book on Resy — opens May 14 (30 days out). This is the night.

Sunday June 14 “Slow Day” add

Sleep in. Morning coffee at The Rise Coffee Bar (5 min walk, garden patio) or Kudu (10 min, secret garden courtyard). Late morning: couples spa at The Dewberry or Woodhouse (see below). Brunch. Explore. Dinner wherever sounds right — maybe Merci (26 seats, James Beard semifinalist) or FIG.

Monday June 15 “Depart” add

One last coffee — Bad Bunnies (8 min walk, perfect 5.0 Yelp, house-made everything, order the brown sugar iced coffee) or Harken Cafe (1 min from hotel, biscuit sandwich + cortado). One last walk. Check out. Head to the airport.
