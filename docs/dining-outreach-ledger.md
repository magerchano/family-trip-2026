## Thanksgiving-meal research (2026-09-12, two passes)

**No Montreal venue offers a traditional Canadian Thanksgiving dinner Oct 10–12, 2026.** Confirmed across official sources, food media, and community forums. Tourisme Montréal's festive-dining roundup has zero Action de grâce entries; Tastet's Thanksgiving list is "restaurants that happen to be open."

**Trap:** Alexandre et fils and Sir Winston Churchill Pub do run full turkey dinners, but for **American** Thanksgiving in late November. They dominate "Montreal Thanksgiving" search results.

**Closed loop on the Fairmont:** forum posters for a decade pointed to **Le Montréalais** at the Queen Elizabeth as the one place doing a Thanksgiving meal. That outlet no longer exists; Rosélys is its successor and has confirmed directly they do not offer one.

**Booking risk, and this matters most:** in Quebec the family celebration is **Sunday**, lunch or dinner, not Monday. Two consequences for Sun Oct 11: independent restaurants have a documented pattern of closing Sunday/Monday of this weekend so staff can be with family, and the ones that stay open see heavier than usual demand. Do not assume normal Sunday service. Confirm open AND able to seat 11.

**Weak leads only:** Marcus at Four Seasons has stated intent for a Thanksgiving brunch edition but nothing published, and its precedent is 9:00am–2:30pm, which collides with DodgeBow at 2:30pm. Bar George at Le Mount Stephen runs a standing All Day Sunday Roast, not a holiday menu, capacity and price unverified. No hotel brunch was recommended by any real poster.
