Tuesday afternoon · the four adults

Brews-day Tuesday

A three-stop beer and nibbles crawl while Nana and the kids do their own thing, timed to land you at FIG by 5pm. Every stop verified open Tuesday.

The Walk

The plan · all on foot
  1. 1:15

    The Griffon 18 Vendue Range

    The real craft anchor: 15+ rotating taps including rare locals, plus fish & chips. Three minutes from the hotel.

  2. 2:20

    Charleston Beer Works 480 King St

    The deep dive: 48 taps plus scratch bar food. A ten-minute stroll up King.

  3. 3:50

    Henry’s rooftop 54 N Market St

    Wind down on the rooftop with City Market views and Lowcountry nibbles. Three minutes from FIG.

  4. 4:50

    Stroll to FIG

    232 Meeting St, in time for the 5:00 reservation. Done, no stress.

About 0.9 miles of walking all afternoon, no cars, and the hard 5pm pin has a three-minute final stroll.

Plan B — The Breweries

Backup · rideshare

If the crew wants actual breweries and doesn’t mind cars up and back. The best beer of the whole search, but it’s a ten to fifteen minute ride to FIG, so order the car by 4:25.

  1. 2:00

    Munkle Brewing 1513 Meeting St Rd

    Belgian-style taproom (opens 2pm). Snacks, or bring your own food.

  2. 3:00

    Edmund’s Oast Brewing 1505 King St Ext

    The standout: best beer plus a full kitchen for nibbles. Four-minute walk from Munkle.

  3. 4:25

    Rideshare to FIG

    Roughly 10 to 15 minutes down Meeting St; pad for evening traffic.

Want a true three-stop? Add Tradesman (1647 King St Ext) at ~1:15 first. Skip-list: Fatty’s and Cooper River are out (Cooper River is closed Tuesdays).
