Conseils & Practical Tips

Key survival tips from Alan's guide with Traz tactical notes to keep parking tickets at zero.

Fee-Free ATMs

Bank of America cardholders get 0% ATM alliance fees at Scotiabank ATMs throughout Montreal. Great for Schwartz's cash!

Traz: "Scotiabank is our free loot vault for BofA."
🚗

Parking & French Signs

Street cleaning and sector parking tickets are brutally expensive. Remember: Lun (Mon), Mar (Tue), Mer (Wed), Jeu (Thu), Ven (Fri), Sam (Sat), Dim (Sun).

Traz: "'Lun-Ven' with an arrow = instant towing trap!"
🚇

Metro & STM Transit

Buses and subway (metro) are super clean and easy. Note: Canadian Thanksgiving Monday (Oct 12) runs on a Sunday holiday schedule.

Traz: "Cleanest transit in North America. Easy metro hops."
🚖

Cabs & Rideshares

Official Montreal taxis won't rip you off. Ubers work everywhere. For 11 people moving to Old Montreal, plan on a 3-Uber convoy.
