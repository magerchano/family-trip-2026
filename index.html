Charleston + Kiawah Island

Michelin stars, dolphin charters, and resort life done right.

The best blend of a true resort stay, easy family logistics, and now, real Michelin credibility. Charleston's Michelin selection shows 15 restaurants, including 3 one-stars and 3 Bib Gourmands. Kiawah gives you a genuine resort setup with villas, kids' pools, dolphin trips, and guided family kayaking.

Nights 1–3

The Charleston Place

The polished city-base move. Downtown Charleston, walkable to everything, two-bedroom suite options for the crew. This is where you feel fancy without trying.

Nights 4–7

Kiawah Island Resort Villa

Switch to full resort mode. A 3+ bedroom villa with resort access means pools, beach, and space to spread out. The Sanctuary is the prestige move, but a villa is smarter for seven people.

Day by Day

Day 1 Arrive & Settle Into Charleston +
Arrive in Charleston, check into The Charleston Place, keep the first night easy. Dinner at Sorelle — Michelin-listed, stylish, and a good "everyone feels like this trip is fancy" opener.
Day 2 The Big Food & City Day +
Your main food-and-city day. For lunch or an easier meal with kids: Leon's Oyster Shop, Lewis Barbecue, or Rodney Scott's BBQ. For the more adult-forward dinner: book Wild Common, Vern's, or Malagón Mercado y Taperia — all Michelin one-star picks. Explore the historic district, Rainbow Row, and the waterfront in between meals.
Day 3 Bulls Island Wildlife Adventure +
Animal and boating day from Charleston with the Bulls Island ferry. The naturalist-led trip includes dolphins, sea turtles, and birdlife on the ride over — a real wildlife note before switching into resort mode. Jax and Rey will love this one.
Day 4 Transfer to Kiawah — Pool Day +
Transfer to Kiawah and go full resort. Beach time, multiple pool complexes including the Night Heron pools with slides and spray features. This is the day Jax and Rey burn off their existence in chlorinated joy while the adults recover with something cold in hand.
Day 5 Private Dolphin Charter +
Book Kiawah's Dolphin Encounters as a private single-party boat. Their charter setup handles groups of 7 easily, and this is one of the cleanest low-hassle private wildlife outings available. The whole family on one boat, dolphins doing their thing.
Day 6 Family Kayaking with Naturalist +
Guided family kayaking with a Kiawah naturalist through Lowcountry marshes. Spot dolphins, birds, and maybe even bobcats. Built for families, not serious paddlers cosplaying as explorers. Easy pace, great scenery, memorable without being exhausting.
Day 7 Beach, Pool & Decompress +
Keep it light: beach, pools, or one of Kiawah's family activities like a sunset cruise or shelling expedition. You already got your wildlife hits — this is the decompression day instead of stuffing in another overplanned "memories" block.
Day 8 Depart (One Last Meal) +
Depart. If there's time, one last Michelin meal: FIG, Lowland, or The Restaurant at Zero George make for a strong final brunch or lunch depending on flight times.
