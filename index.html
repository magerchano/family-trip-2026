The Week

Monday through Saturday — the family trip

Monday June 8 “Arrive & Settle In” +

Arrive in Charleston, check into Mill House. Keep the first night easy — walk the neighborhood, get oriented, casual dinner. Consider Leon’s Oyster Shop or Poogan’s Porch for an easy opener that works for everyone.

Tuesday June 9 “The Big Food & City Day” +

The main exploring day. Historic district, Rainbow Row, the Battery, waterfront. Lunch at one of the buzzier spots (Chubby Fish if you’re willing to line up, or Xiao Bao Biscuit for something easier). Michelin dinner for the adults if Jan’s up for babysitting, or a family-friendly spot like Rodney Scott’s.

Wednesday June 10 “Kiawah Island Day Trip” +

Day trip to Kiawah for beach, pools, and a dolphin charter or family kayaking. Book Kiawah’s Dolphin Encounters as a single-party boat — their charter setup handles groups of 7 easily. Back to Charleston for dinner.

Thursday June 11 “Oyster Farm & Water Day” +

Charleston Oyster Farm tour in the morning (charlestonoysterfarm.com). Afternoon at the beach or pool. Evening: try one of the TikTok-viral spots — Fleet Landing for waterfront seafood or Darling Oyster Bar.

Friday June 12 “Bulls Island or Flex Day” +

Option A: Bulls Island ferry — naturalist-led trip with dolphins, sea turtles, and birdlife. Jax and Rey will love this. Option B: Flex day — pool, shopping on King Street, or revisit a favorite spot. Last family dinner — make it a good one.

Saturday June 13 “Family Departs” +

Family heads out. Michael and Haley check into the couples weekend hotel. Decompress. Evening: special dinner at Wild Common (Michelin 1-star tasting menu) or Vern’s.

The Couples Weekend

Saturday Evening “The Upgrade” +

Check into the couples hotel. Dinner at a starred restaurant — Wild Common, Vern’s, or Malagón. This is the night to go all out.

Sunday June 14 “Slow Day” +

Sleep in. Coffee walk (see coffee guide below). Brunch. Explore at your own pace. No agenda, no kids, no schedule. Dinner wherever sounds right — maybe Merci (26 seats, James Beard semifinalist) or FIG.

Monday June 15 “Depart” +

One last coffee, one last walk. Head to the airport.
